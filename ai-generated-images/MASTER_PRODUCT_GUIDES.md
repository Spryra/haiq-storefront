## How to Use This Document

1. **Pick a product** from the sections above
2. **Copy the relevant SCENE prompts** (1–6 for cookies, 1–6 for boxes)
3. **Paste into Google AI Studio**
4. **Set aspect ratio** as specified in the section header
5. **Generate 2–3 variations** per scene
6. **Save to the product folder** following naming convention

---

## Master Naming Convention

```
{product-slug}-{scene}-variation-{number}.png
```

**Examples:**
```
coconut-hero-variation-1.png
crimson-sin-overhead-variation-2.png
campfire-after-dark-broken-variation-1.png
blackout-flatlay-variation-1.png

```
