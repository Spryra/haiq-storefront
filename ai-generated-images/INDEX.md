## ✅ How to Generate All At Once

### Setup (One Time)
1. Go to [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com)
2. Settings:
   - Model: `Imagen 4 Ultra`
   - Images per run: `4`
   - Quality: `2K`
   - Format: `PNG`

### Per Product (Batch Workflow)
1. Pick a product (e.g., **Coconut**)
2. Open `MASTER_PRODUCT_GUIDES.md` → search "COCONUT"
3. For each scene (hero, overhead, broken, etc.):
   - Copy the PROMPT
   - Set the correct ASPECT RATIO
   - Paste NEGATIVE prompt
   - Click **Generate**
   - Choose best of 4
   - Download as PNG
   - Rename: `coconut-hero-variation-1.png`
   - Save to: `/coconut/hero/`
4. Repeat for all 6 scenes
5. Move to next product

### Total Time Estimate
- **Per product (1 variation per scene):** 20–30 minutes
- **Per product (2–3 variations per scene):** 40–60 minutes
- **All 7 products (1 variation each):** ~3.5 hours
- **All 7 products (2–3 variations each):** ~6–7 hours

**Recommended:** Generate 2 variations per scene for flexibility.

## ✨ Quality Checklist (For All Products)

Before saving each image:
- [ ] No text, watermark, or logo visible
- [ ] Product shape matches product type (cookie is domed, box is cubic)
- [ ] Colours are accurate to product (Venom = dark, Coconut = light, etc.)
- [ ] Background is pure matte black
- [ ] Single directional light creates proper shadows
- [ ] Sharp focus on product detail
- [ ] Aspect ratio is correct (1:1, 4:3, or 16:9)
- [ ] No visible hands, tools, or unwanted objects
- [ ] No cartoon or illustration style

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Batch by product:** Generate all 6 scenes for one product, then move to the next
2. **Reuse seeds:** Lock a successful seed and use it for variations with minor prompt tweaks
3. **Generate 2–3 variations:** Gives you choices when uploading to Cloudinary
4. **Quality over quantity:** 4K PNG is better than multiple compressed JPEGs
5. **Consistent lighting:** All products use single key light from upper-left for cohesion
